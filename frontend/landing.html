How TruthKeeper works

Understand → Trust → Decide → Act

  1. 01 · Understand

    Ask across verified knowledge

    Ask a question or run a full research brief. Every answer is grounded in cited sources — and withheld when the source can't be trusted.

  2. 02 · Trust

    Audit for contradictions & rot

    Gemini interrogates every document, scores confidence, cites evidence, and maps how your knowledge connects and conflicts.

  3. 03 · Decide

    Humans stay in control

    Review findings and approve or dismiss suggested fixes. Every decision is logged — who decided what, and when.

  4. 04 · Act

    Remediate and verify

    Turn a problem into a correction, preview the change, approve it, and watch the document get re-verified as healthy.
